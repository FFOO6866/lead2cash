# Marine Sales Intelligence - Insight Structure Requirements

## Purpose

This document defines the required structure for marine sales intelligence insights
generated by the InsightsService. All insights MUST follow this structure to be
actionable for the RRPS sales team.

## Target Audience

RRPS (Rolls-Royce Power Systems) sales team selling marine engines in:
- **Priority Regions**: Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Australia, China, Korea, Japan, India
- **Priority Sectors**: Marine transportation (ferries, tugs, harbour craft), Offshore oil & gas (OSVs, FPSOs), Marine engineering (shipyards, MRO)

## Sales Signals (Signal Types)

Each opportunity MUST be tagged with one or more of these signals:

| Signal | Description | Sales Relevance |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| `NEWBUILD` | New vessel orders, shipyard contracts | Primary engine sales opportunity |
| `RETROFIT_REPOWER` | Engine replacement, upgrade projects | Replacement engine opportunity |
| `OFFSHORE_PROJECT` | Oil & gas FID, EPCIC contracts | OSV/FPSO vessel demand |
| `REGULATION` | IMO emissions, MPA requirements | Compliance-driven upgrades |
| `FUEL_TRANSITION` | LNG, methanol, ammonia conversions | Dual-fuel engine opportunity |
| `FLEET_EXPANSION` | Operator fleet growth | Multiple engine opportunity |
| `INCIDENT_RELIABILITY` | Engine failures, breakdowns | Emergency replacement opportunity |
| `FINANCING_CAPEX` | Major capital announcements | Budget availability signal |

## Output Format Examples

The output is designed to be **clean, scannable, and organized** for busy sales professionals.

### News Briefing Format

```markdown
## 🇸🇬 Singapore

### Regulatory & Official

**MPA Updates Harbor Craft Emission Standards**
Tier III requirements | All vessels in Singapore port waters by 2027.
[Read more → MPA Singapore](https://mpa.gov.sg/news/emission-standards)

**Seatrium Reports $2.1B Order Book**
$2.1B | Q4 results strong. 3 new contracts including FPSO work for Petrobras.
[Read more → SGX Filing](https://sgx.com/filings/seatrium-q4)

### Industry News

**SBS Transit Orders 4 Hybrid Ferries**
$33M | 4 ferry(s) | Contract awarded to Penguin Shipyard for 400-passenger hybrid ferries.
[Read more → Maritime Executive](https://maritime-executive.com/article/sbs-ferry)

**Marco Polo Marine Wins 2 Offshore Contracts**
$18M | 2 OSV(s) | Platform supply vessel contracts for Indonesian operations.
[Read more → TradeWinds](https://tradewinds.com/offshore/marco-polo)

### Company Announcements

**Seatrium Delivers First LNG-Powered Tug to PSA**
LNG tug | Singapore's first LNG harbor tug enters service at Pasir Panjang.
[Read more → Seatrium](https://seatrium.com/news/lng-tug-psa)
```

### Format Rules

Each news item has exactly 3 lines:
1. **Bold headline** - What happened
2. Brief description - Key details (value, vessel count, one sentence of context)
3. Source link - `[Read more → Source Name](url)`

### Grouping

News is organized by:
1. **Region** (Singapore first)
2. **Source category** within each region:
   - Regulatory & Official (MPA, PSA, SMF, SGX filings)
   - Industry News (Maritime Executive, TradeWinds, Lloyd's List)
   - Company Announcements (Seatrium, shipyards, operators)

## Data Quality Scoring

Insights are scored 0-100 based on data completeness:

| Field | Weight | Scoring |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Company names (operator + shipyard) | 25 | Both = 25, One = 15, None = 0 |
| Contract value | 20 | Exact = 20, Estimated = 10, None = 0 |
| Vessel specifications | 20 | Full = 20, Partial = 10, None = 0 |
| Engine requirements | 15 | Detailed = 15, Basic = 8, None = 0 |
| Timeline | 10 | Specific = 10, Approximate = 5, None = 0 |
| Source reliability | 10 | Primary = 10, Secondary = 5, Rumor = 0 |

**Score Interpretation:**
- **85-100**: High confidence, actionable immediately
- **60-84**: Medium confidence, verify key details
- **40-59**: Low confidence, needs research
- **<40**: Insufficient data, do not pursue without verification
